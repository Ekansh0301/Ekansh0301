### Hi, I'm Ekansh

I'm a student at IIIT Hyderabad who learns best by building. When something catches my interest, whether it's a database, an operating system or a language model, my first instinct is to open an empty file and try writing my own version. The parts that break usually teach me the most.

I'm drawn to problems with tight constraints: making something fit into too little memory, keep running when pieces of it fail, or work entirely offline on hardware that was never meant for it. I like getting a thing to work, and then understanding exactly why it does.

Away from the keyboard, I'm usually on a cricket field.
